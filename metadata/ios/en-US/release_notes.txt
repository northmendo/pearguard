## What's Changed

Improvements
  - On computers with more than one monitor, the block screen now covers every screen. A blocked app can no longer be dragged to a second monitor and used there.
  - PearGuard now restarts itself if it is closed or freezes on a child's Linux computer. It also restarts after a freeze on Windows, where before it only restarted if it was closed.

Bug Fixes
  - Fixed a rare problem that could stop PearGuard from working in the background after saving data.
